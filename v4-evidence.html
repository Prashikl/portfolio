How I work

Security-first design: pausing an unsafe account-data change

I spotted that a proposed account-data change lacked adequate authorization, consent, notification, and auditability, and reframed it as a customer and agreement-integrity risk. The feature was paused until a safer system model could be designed.

Strategic enterprise-customer controls under pressure

I translated a need for transparency, role segregation, control, and enforcement into specific experience changes on a tight timeline, and the work was delivered on schedule.

Healthy conflict with engineering

I made the user outcome and the business rule explicit, asked what technical constraints meant for behavior, explored alternatives, and documented trade-offs. Internal recognition confirms that healthy disagreement improved the ordering platform.

Priority shift and resilience

When the portfolio shifted, I captured reusable domain knowledge, closed the old work explicitly, mapped the new system and its stakeholders, and ramped quickly into newer enterprise commerce work. Commitment to the outcome outranks attachment to a roadmap.

Raising the bar on delivery discipline

Parallel ordering work and a vendor dependency stacked up delivery risk. I came out of it with a sharper operating rhythm: explicit estimates, milestone plans, decision and hurdle logs, named owners, a review cadence, early escalation, and a clear definition of done.

Mentoring and multiplying others

For a vendor designer supporting Credits, I clarified shared patterns, supplied domain context, reviewed flows and states, gave actionable feedback, and stepped in when delivery risk emerged.

AI-assisted design execution

I use approved AI workflows for research synthesis, IA exploration, copy and voice checks, rapid prototypes, documentation pipelines, and handoff checklists, which saves me roughly 30% on content review and handoff prep. Sensitive data stays out of unapproved tools, and generated output is checked against evidence and rules.

Leadership storytelling

I create Sankey-style flows, short videos, diagrams, and decision-focused narratives that connect user pain, dependencies, approach, and outcome. My manager credited these artifacts and coaching with improving executive readiness.
