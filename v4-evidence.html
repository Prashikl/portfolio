Portfolio rule

Lead with the user decision and product consequence.

Every story separates the problem, personal role, evidence that changed the work, consequential decision, and result or current status. Individual contribution is never presented as sole programme causality.

Verified shipped

Supported by project records, release evidence, performance material, or stakeholder recognition.

Verified contribution

Ownership or participation is supported, but a final business result may not be available.

Self-reported

A metric appears in performance material; baseline, denominator, period, and instrument must be reopened.

In flight

Designed, planned, piloted, or being evaluated. Language stays directional.

Stopped

Work ended before delivery and is used as a learning or resilience story.

Validate

Source conflicts, missing telemetry, or unclear release status remain visible.

Supporting stories

Security-first design: pause an unsafe account-data change

I identified that a proposed account-data change lacked adequate authorization, consent, notification, and auditability. I reframed it as customer and agreement-integrity risk; the feature was paused until a safer system model could be designed. Pilot timing and formal decision evidence remain to validate.

Strategic enterprise-customer controls under pressure

Translated a need for transparency, role segregation, control, and enforcement into specific experience changes under tight timelines. The source reports on-schedule delivery; broader support reduction remains directional.

Healthy conflict with engineering

Made the user outcome and business rule explicit, asked what technical constraints meant for behavior, explored alternatives, and documented trade-offs. Internal recognition confirms that healthy disagreement improved the ordering platform.

Priority shift and resilience

After responding too slowly to a portfolio shift, I captured reusable domain knowledge, explicitly closed old work, mapped the new system and stakeholders, and ramped into newer enterprise commerce work. Commitment to outcome must be stronger than attachment to a roadmap.

Planning and escalation failure

Parallel ordering work and a vendor dependency accumulated delivery and quality risk. I did not escalate early enough, absorbed work late, and weakened stakeholder confidence. I adopted explicit estimates, milestone plans, decision and hurdle logs, owners, review cadence, early escalation, and a definition of done.

Mentoring and multiplying others

For a vendor designer supporting Credits, I clarified shared patterns, supplied domain context, reviewed flows and states, gave actionable feedback, and stepped in when delivery risk emerged. A concrete independent-success example remains to collect.

AI-assisted design execution

Used approved AI workflows for research synthesis, IA exploration, copy and voice checks, rapid prototypes, documentation pipelines, and handoff checklists. Personal efficiency estimates remain qualified; sensitive data stays out of unapproved tools and generated output is checked against evidence and rules.

Leadership storytelling

Created Sankey-style flows, short videos, diagrams, and decision-focused narratives connecting user pain, dependencies, approach, and outcome. Manager feedback credited these artifacts and coaching with improving executive readiness; the original feedback and a sanitized visual remain to collect.

Validation

Global publication checklist

  • Verify exact dates, phase names, and production status.
  • Reopen every metric source and complete its evidence worksheet.
  • Confirm research sample, method, date, and the decision each finding changed.
  • Separate personal ownership from product, engineering, research, operations, and business outcomes.
  • Collect one real rejected alternative and one real edge case per flagship story.
  • Collect UAT, bug, accessibility, release, telemetry, support, or stakeholder evidence.
  • Review screenshots and fields for confidential data, identifiers, and internal URLs.
  • Use public-safe labels where approval is uncertain and expand acronyms only when confirmed.
  • Have technical and research partners review accuracy.

Minimum evidence per case

One problem signal, one model that changed understanding, one rejected alternative, one collaboration record, one delivery artifact, one outcome signal or measurement plan, and one behavior or process that changed.
