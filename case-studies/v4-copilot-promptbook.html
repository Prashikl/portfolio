01 Premise

AI should accelerate sense-making, not outsource judgement.

Dense specifications, research, seller evidence, business rules, and meeting decisions created translation loss between research, design, and implementation. I used approved AI-assisted tools to make that material reusable, explore more alternatives, reveal edge states, and give PM and engineering working behavior to inspect earlier.

Verified personal contribution

Copilot, Copilot Notebooks, Markdown workflows, VS Code, Claude, synthesis, IA and flow exploration, copy refinement, gap identification, scenario building, and interactive Commerce Insights/MCAE prototypes.

Claim audit

“Commerce Insights was built entirely in VS Code” is not supported. The safe claim is AI-assisted context pipelines and VS Code prototypes; engineering retained production accountability.

02 Workflow

Build the scaffolding around the model.

01

Prepare approved evidence

Scope specifications, research synthesis, JTBDs, seller evidence, decisions, screenshots, current code, and business rules.

02

Structure reusable context

Turn fragmented material into notebooks, Markdown context, action records, and stable naming.

03

Explore before converging

Compare IA, flows, content, edge cases, error states, and prototype plans without inventing product logic.

04

Build working behavior

Use an AI-assisted coding environment and fictional data to test interactive alternatives, not just static frames.

05

Inspect and review

Check component reuse, states, responsiveness, accessibility, assumptions, and business rules with PM, research, design, and engineering.

06

Hand off with limits

Share the approved prototype, decision record, context, acceptance criteria, and known gaps; production stays cross-functional.

03 Prompt design

Treat a prompt as an interaction contract.

Intent

Define entry conditions

Name persona, job, trigger, available context, and desired outcome; ask when the goal is missing.

Hierarchy

Shape the response

Specify priority, sections, confidence labels, and evidence links; separate facts from hypotheses.

Status

Represent system state

Expose loading, tools, partial completion, routing, failure, and long-running work honestly.

Safety

Constrain and recover

Bound sources and tools; support retry, refine, edit, fallback, escalation, and preserved work.

Reuse

Version stable guidance

Distinguish maintained instructions and domain references from temporary conversation state.

Evaluation

Test scenarios, not vibes

Use golden prompts, variants, edge cases, adversarial asks, expected output, and rubrics.

04 Trust + quality

Human gates turn speed into accountable progress.

I reviewed generated work against source evidence, user intent, product rules, and design standards. Sensitive data stayed out of unapproved tools; consequential actions required consent and review. The wider quality model covered groundedness, task completeness, routing, retrieval, schema correctness, regression, latency, trust and control, accessibility, adoption, and safe failure.

Personal principle

Human accountability, source verification, approved tools, explicit uncertainty, and business-rule checks.

Team control

Sequential review, PM/research/design/engineering checks, and production UAT, security, accessibility, RAI, and release gates.

Personal estimate

About 30% less content-review and handoff-preparation time in a 2025 self-report; a later 30–50% broader estimate remains directional, not a product KPI.

Missing proof

Baseline, sample period, calculation, rejected AI suggestion, personal accessibility/RAI closure, and individual evaluation sheets.

Access-control routing suggests stewardship; edit history and authorship are not established.

Adjacent evidence

Agent trust and orchestration

Deal, Commerce, Data, Quote, history, and book patterns shaped quality thinking; direct delivery ownership is not claimed.

Programme only

Assistant metrics

Adoption, prompt volume, completion, routing, and latency figures belong to programme teams, not my impact.

06 Evidence + next

Traceability is the next maturity step.

The career arc moves from learning and copy/handoff assistance, to structured context pipelines and prototypes, to scenario, orchestration, and quality thinking. The next step is a dated inventory linking artefact, owner, version, source permissions, evaluation result, and approved reuse.

  • Confirm exact prototype repositories, commits, and tool use by stage.
  • Inventory personally authored prompts, notebooks, instructions, skills, agents, and guideline files.
  • Verify shared-library submissions, experimentation outputs, prompt/scenario edit history, and personal UAT or support records.
  • Map every agent item to personal contribution versus programme context.
  • Replace internal names, links, identifiers, data, and metrics with approved public representations.
The design problem is not what a model can generate. It is what conditions make its behavior useful, inspectable, and responsible.
